Speakwell — Technical Product Architecture & Specification

Mission: Disrupting expensive 1:1 human executive speech coaching ($150+/hr) by building an intelligent, authentic speech rehearsal platform that empowers anyone to speak freely and sound sharp.

1. Product Philosophy & Vision

Traditional speech coaching charges $150–$300 per hour, while standard AI writing tools rewrite speech into robotic corporate jargon ("synergistic", "delve into", "paradigm shift").

Speakwell operates on the Authentic Voice Philosophy:

  • 100% Personal Voice Preservation: Preserves the speaker's exact vocabulary, stories, personal style, and tone.
  • Friction Elimination: Strips away verbal hesitation, filler words ("um", "uh", "like"), false starts, and run-on thoughts without altering identity.
  • Mental Thought Structuring: Helps speakers map raw thoughts into coherent, persuasive structural frameworks.

4. Universal Zero-Duplication Speech Engine

Speech recognition on WebKit and mobile browsers (iOS Safari, Android Chrome) frequently re-emits accumulated speech buffers, causing compounding word duplication ("I think I think what I think what one...").

Speakwell resolves this permanently using a Universal 4-Stage Zero-Duplication Engine (speechEngine.js):

  1. Stage 1 (Single Word): Strips consecutive duplicate words ("word word" → "word").
  2. Stage 2 (N-Gram Phrase): Strips 2-to-10 word repeating phrase loops ("one understands by one understands by" → "one understands by").
  3. Stage 3 (Rolling Window Collapse): Token array scanner detecting and collapsing cumulative WebKit buffer overlays.
  4. Stage 4 (Boundary Stitching): Stitches overlapping phrase endings cleanly ("I think what" + "what one understands" → "I think what one understands").

5. Measurement Metrics & Scoring Engine

Every speech session is evaluated across 6 quantitative metrics and 2 qualitative thought mappings:

Quantitative Parameters (0-100)
• Overall Score: Weighted composition.
• WPM Cadence: Ideal 130–150 WPM.
• Clarity %: Sentence structure precision.
• Speech Flow %: Rhythm & transitions.
• Filler Counter: um, uh, like, you know.
• Grammar & Pause Balance: Syntactical accuracy.
Qualitative Thought Mapping
• Structural Thought Blueprint 📐: Maps speech into 📌 Core Point, 💡 Key Rationale, 🎯 Evidence, and 🏁 Wrap-Up Takeaway.
• 🌟 Strongest Landed Point: Highlights the single highest-impact sentence.

6. Document Sanitizer Engine

When uploading presentation decks or PDFs into Script Rehearsal, raw extraction often introduces slide junk lines. Speakwell handles document edge cases via scriptSanitizer.js:

  • Table of Contents Filter: Strips index number sequences (1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8 9 and TABLE OF CONTENTS).
  • Footer/Header Stripper: Filters slide footers (-Author Name), page numbers (Page 1 of 12), and copyright metadata.
  • Bullet Symbol Cleaner: Replaces slide bullet symbols (●, ▪, ◆, ➢) with clean punctuation and continuous spoken paragraph flow.
  • Interactive Section Filter: Groups documents into selectable section cards with 1-tap toggling and 1-click 🪄 Auto-Clean into Speech Script.
